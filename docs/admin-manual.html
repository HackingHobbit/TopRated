1  Getting Started

The admin portal is where you run the store: manage stock and prices, list individual cards, review customers and orders, add staff, and connect payments. It lives at /admin and is visible only to accounts with the admin role.

1.1  Signing in

  1. Go to the store and open Sign In (the /login page).
  2. Enter the email and password for your admin account.
  3. Once signed in, an Admin button (with a dashboard icon) appears in the top navigation bar. Click it to enter the portal.
Access is enforced on the server You must be both signed in and an admin to see any admin page. Visitors who aren't logged in are sent to the sign-in page (and returned to /admin afterwards); signed-in customers who aren't admins are redirected to their account page. The "Admin" button in the top bar simply doesn't appear for non-admins.

1.2  Understanding roles

RoleWhat it can do
AdminFull access to the admin portal and every screen in this manual. This is the role for you and your staff.
CustomerA normal shopper account. Can browse, order, and see their own account — but never the admin portal.

You promote or demote accounts between these two roles on the Users screen (Section 7).

2  Daily Dashboard Live data

The landing screen. It shows today's date and four headline numbers pulled from the real store database, followed by your most recent order activity.

2.1  The four metric cards

CardWhat it counts
Sales TodayThe total dollar value of all orders placed since midnight today. The sub-line shows how many orders that is.
Orders TodayThe number of orders placed since midnight.
Out of StockHow many products are currently flagged out of stock. Reads "All in stock" when the count is zero, or "Needs restock" when it isn't.
CustomersThe total number of registered customer accounts.

2.2  Recent Activity

Below the cards, a table lists your six most recent orders — order number, customer name (taken from the shipping details, or "Guest" for guest checkouts), a colored status badge (Pending, Processing, or Shipped), and the amount. If no orders exist yet, it simply reads "No orders yet."

2.3  The "Demo mode" tools

Only appears while payments are in Mock mode When the Clover payment integration is set to Mock (its default — see Section 8), a small Demo mode badge and a Delete demo orders button appear near the top of the dashboard. This lets you clear out test checkouts so they don't clutter your real figures. It deletes only demo orders (those whose number starts with DEMO-) — never a real customer order — and asks you to confirm first. Once you switch payments to Live, this tool disappears.
Use it as your daily open Check the dashboard first thing: glance at Sales/Orders Today, and if "Out of Stock" says "Needs restock," head to Inventory to see what needs attention.

3  Inventory Live data

The main catalog — your sealed boxes, singles from the bulk import, and supplies. Here you search the whole catalog, flip the flags that control how items appear on the storefront, and edit an item's name, description, price, and image.

The header shows the section title, the subtitle "Manage Inventory, Prices, and Store Flags," and a Total Items count so you always know how big the catalog is.

3.3  Editing a product

Click Edit on any row to open the editor. You can change:

  • Name and Description
  • Price (in dollars)
  • Image — a live preview is shown. Use the Search for Image button to find a picture, or the small info (i) button to reveal a field where you can paste an image link directly.
  • Representative image checkbox — tick this when the photo is a close stand-in rather than the exact item; shoppers then see a "Representative image" note.

Click Save Changes to apply, or Cancel to discard.

Adding & deleting on this screen The main Inventory list is fed by your bulk catalog and is built for maintaining existing products — it doesn't have "Add" or "Delete" buttons. To create and remove individual cards yourself, use Custom Inventory (Section 4).

4  Custom Inventory — Singles Live data

This is where you list individual cards yourself — snap a photo, fill in the details, and it's on the store. It's the most hands-on screen and the one designed to be used from your phone at the counter.

The header reads "Custom Inventory" with a subtitle counting how many singles are listed. An Add Single button sits at top right.

What counts as a "single" A single is a non-sealed trading card in a card category (sports or TCG) — an individual card, not a sealed box, accessory, or supply item. Items you add here show up grouped by category.

4.1  Browsing your singles

  • A search box filters by card name or category as you type.
  • Cards are grouped by category, and each group header shows how many are in it.
  • Each row shows a thumbnail (or a "No photo" placeholder), the card's name, its subcategory and price, and an "Out of stock" note if flagged.
  • Row actions: the pencil opens the editor; the trash deletes the card (and its photos) after a confirmation.

4.2  Adding a single

Click Add Single. The form is organized top-to-bottom so the fastest path is simply to take a photo and fill in three required fields.

Photos (the top section)

The photo tools come first because they're the part that should feel effortless on a phone:

ButtonWhat it does
Take PhotoOn a phone, opens the rear camera directly — shoot the card and it uploads instantly.
Add PhotosOpens your photo library / file picker; you can select several at once.
Search for ImageFinds a stock/reference image online if you'd rather not shoot one.
Drag & dropOn a desktop, drag image files straight onto the drop zone.

Uploaded photos appear as thumbnails. The first photo is the Primary (used as the store thumbnail); on any other photo, click Make primary to promote it, or the × to remove it. Photos are automatically shrunk before uploading so large phone pictures don't slow things down. A Representative photo checkbox marks the image as a stand-in rather than the exact card.

4.3  Editing or deleting a single

Click the pencil on a row to reopen the same form pre-filled with everything you entered — including its photos — and change anything, then Save Changes. Click the trash to delete; you'll be asked to confirm, and this removes the card and its photos permanently.

Counter workflow A new card in the case? On your phone: Add Single → Take Photo → type the Title, pick the Category, enter a Price → Add Single. It's live in under a minute.

7  Users & Staff Live data

The User & Staff Management screen is where accounts are actually created, edited, promoted, and removed. This is your control room for who can do what.

7.1  The toolbar

  • Search — filter the user list by name or email.
  • Add User — create a new account (see 7.2).
If "Add User" is greyed out Creating and deleting accounts requires an extra server key (the Supabase service-role key) to be configured. If it isn't, the Add User button and the delete action are disabled, and a notice explains this — but role changes, profile edits, and password resets still work without it.

7.2  Adding a user

  1. Click Add User.
  2. Enter an Email and a Name.
  3. Set a Temporary password (minimum 6 characters) — the person can change it later.
  4. Choose a Role: customer, or admin (staff) for someone who needs the portal.
  5. Click Create account. (The button stays disabled until the email looks valid and the password is long enough.)

7.3  Managing existing users

Each row in the table offers:

ControlWhat it does
Role dropdownSwitch a person between customer and admin. The change takes effect immediately.
Edit (pencil)Open a small dialog to change their Name and Loyalty points.
Password reset (key)Emails the user a password-reset link.
Delete (trash)Permanently removes the account after a confirmation.

The table also shows each person's loyalty points and the date they joined. Your own account is marked with a small "you" tag, and — as a safety measure — you can't delete yourself.

Onboarding a new staff member Add User → set their email, name, and a temporary password → choose admin (staff) → Create account. Tell them to use the Password reset link (or the reset key on their row) to set their own password.

8  Integrations — Clover Payments Setup

The Integrations screen connects the store to Clover for taking card payments. The whole thing runs off one switch: Mock (simulate everything, no account needed) or Live (real payments with real credentials).

8.1  Reading the status banner

A banner at the top tells you what's currently active:

BannerMeaning
Phantom (mock) CloverPayments are being simulated. This is the default and is safe — no real money moves.
LIVE CloverReal Clover payments are active.
Set to Live but falling back to mockYou chose Live but a required credential (Merchant ID or API token) is missing, so it's safely staying in mock. Fill in the missing fields to go truly live.
Settings table not foundA one-time database migration hasn't been run yet, so saving and the Live toggle aren't available. Ask your developer to run it.

8.2  The Mock / Live switch

  • Mock (phantom) — everything is simulated end-to-end. Leave it here while you set up or demo the store.
  • Live — flip to this only once you've entered and tested real Clover credentials.

8.3  The credential fields

FieldWhat it is
EnvironmentSandbox (testing) or Production (real money). Always start with Sandbox.
Merchant ID (mId)Your Clover merchant identifier.
Platform API TokenYour Clover platform token. Stored securely — once set, it shows masked, and you can leave it blank to keep the current one.
Ecommerce Public Key (PAKMS)The browser-side key used to tokenize card details.
Ecommerce Private KeyThe server-side key used to charge cards. Also stored masked.
Where to find these in Clover In your Clover Dashboard: Setup → API Tokens gives you the Merchant ID and Platform token; Settings → Ecommerce → API Tokens gives you the public and private ecommerce keys. Use the Sandbox environment first.

8.4  Testing and saving

  1. Enter your credentials.
  2. Click Test Connection — a success message confirms the keys work (and shows your merchant name); a failure explains what's wrong.
  3. Click Save Settings.
  4. Only after a successful test in Sandbox should you switch Environment to Production and the mode to Live.
Until Clover is Live, the store can't charge real cards In Mock mode, checkouts are simulated. Complete this screen with real Production credentials — and test them — before relying on the store to take real payments.

10  Troubleshooting & Glossary

10.1  Common issues

SymptomWhat to do
I don't see the "Admin" button in the top barYou're either not signed in or your account isn't an admin. Sign in with an admin account; if you should be an admin, have another admin promote you on the Users screen.
Going to /admin sends me to the sign-in or account pageSame cause — the server only lets signed-in admins in. Sign in as an admin.
"Add User" is greyed outThe server needs the Supabase service-role key configured to create/delete accounts. Role changes, edits, and password resets still work meanwhile. Ask your developer to set the key.
A flag toggle failedYou'll see a brief error. Check your connection and click the toggle again.
Custom Inventory rows show "No photo"Those items were listed without an image. Open the row's editor and add a photo.
Integrations says "Settings table not found"A one-time database migration hasn't been run. Payments stay in safe Mock mode until your developer runs it.
Real orders don't appear on the Orders screenExpected for now — that screen is a preview. Real orders show on the Dashboard's "Recent Activity."
